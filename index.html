CASE STUDY

        
Full-Funnel B2B Lead Engine — Payroll & Benefits, UAE

        
The Problem

        
The company sells WPS-compliant payroll solutions and salary cards to businesses across the UAE. The target: HR managers, finance directors, and CFOs at SMEs and enterprises. When I joined, the marketing function had channels running but no system connecting them. Leads came in with no scoring, no qualification SOP, and no attribution. The workable rate sat at 16% — meaning 84 out of every 100 leads were junk.

        
What I Built

        
I rebuilt the entire lead generation engine from the ground up.

        
Channels: Restructured Google Ads (Search, PMax, Display), launched LinkedIn Ads for B2B targeting, ran Meta campaigns with MENA VPN segmentation, and built a DV360 programmatic layer. Managed SEO with 60+ articles targeting UAE payroll and WPS compliance keywords.

        
Qualification: Defined lead stages in Salesforce — from raw lead to MQL to SQL to closed-won. Built scoring rules, created qualification SOPs for the telesales team, and established bi-weekly pipeline reviews with Sales.

        
Automation: Automated 80% of CRM workflows in Salesforce. Built email nurture sequences in Customer.io, mobile engagement via CleverTap, and lead capture with OptinMonster. Created cross-platform dashboards in GA4, Google Ads, Search Console, and Looker Studio.

        
Referral GTM: Launched a referral program across the entire 20,000-client base — email campaigns, portal integration, exit popups, email signature CTAs, and non-responder automation. Hit 46 leads/month at a 65% qualification rate.

        
The Result

        
Lead workable rate: 16% → 48% (3× lift in 2 years). Qualified-to-closed-won rate: 37% → 52%. 80% of CRM workflows fully automated. Referral channel producing 46 qualified leads per month. Promoted from Specialist to Performance Marketing Manager.

        
Stack: Google Ads · LinkedIn · Meta · DV360 · Salesforce · GA4 · Customer.io · CleverTap · OptinMonster · Unbounce · Looker Studio

      


      

        
CASE STUDY

        
Referral GTM — Zero to 46 Leads/Month

        
The Problem

        
The business had 20,000+ active payroll clients. Not one of them had ever been asked for a referral. The company had no referral infrastructure — no tracking, no incentive structure, no outreach. An entire channel sitting at zero.

        
What I Built

        
Designed and launched the full referral GTM in 8 weeks. Built the tracking layer with Google Tag Manager events firing on referral form submissions. Set up CleverTap for mobile push notifications to existing clients. Created OptinMonster exit-intent popups on the client portal. Added referral CTAs to every employee email signature across the company. Built a non-responder automation sequence — if a client didn't engage with the first ask, a re-engagement flow triggered at 7 and 21 days via Customer.io.

        
The Result

        
Went from zero referral leads to 46 per month within four months. 65% of referral leads qualified — the highest qualification rate of any channel, because referred leads come pre-validated by someone who already uses the product. The program became self-sustaining with minimal ongoing budget.

        
Stack: CleverTap · OptinMonster · Customer.io · GTM · Salesforce

      


      

        
CASE STUDY

        
Budget Ownership at Scale — HR Services, UAE

        
The Problem

        
Two UAE-based HR services companies running paid media without structured optimization. Budgets were being spent, but ROI attribution was thin, SEO was an afterthought, and campaign experiments were ad-hoc rather than systematic.

        
What I Built

        
At the larger of the two, I took ownership of an AED 2M annual budget across Google Ads and paid social (Facebook, Instagram, YouTube). Rebuilt campaign structures for optimal budget allocation. Implemented an SEO strategy alongside PPC — the two reinforced each other, with PPC data informing organic keyword priorities and organic rankings reducing CPC for branded terms.

        
At the other, I ran the full optimization cycle — PPC restructuring, display campaign management, and a systematic SEO push. Managed external media agencies and vendors. Ran conversion experiments continuously rather than as one-off tests.

        
The Result

        
Achieved #1 ranking on 15+ competitive keywords. Reached #1 Alexa rank in the HR services category — ahead of every competitor. Earlier in my career, at an IT services company, I delivered a 300% ROI improvement for eCommerce operations and hit #1 SEO rank in the USA and Canada for competitive app-development keywords.

        
Stack: Google Ads · Meta Ads · YouTube Ads · GA · SEMrush · Google Tag Manager
